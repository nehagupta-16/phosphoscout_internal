Overview

What the variant is and why it's being analyzed

Mutation (CDS)
Mutation (AA)p.A765T

OGT p.Ala765Thr (A765T) is reported among ClinVar submissions for OGT variants [4]. Pathogenic OGT variants are associated with an X-linked intellectual disability syndrome (OGT-CDG), underscoring the gene’s neurodevelopmental relevance [2, 3]. Within the analyzed 29-mer, A765T introduces a threonine-centered, solvent-accessible potential phosphoacceptor site at position 765 supported by phosphorylation-site database analysis [6]. In silico kinase specificity scoring at this site indicates increased recognition probabilities for several serine/threonine kinases (ACVR1B, ACVR2A, ACVR2B, BMPR1A, BMPR1B) in the mutant relative to wildtype [8].

Hypotheses

Mechanistic explanations generated from the overview and literature.

OGT p.A765T creates a novel T765 phosphosite that is predicted to gain BMP/TGFβ receptor kinase recognition, potentially rewiring OGT regulation to impair neurodevelopment in OGT-CDG

OGT p.A765T creates a new, solvent-accessible threonine phosphoacceptor at center_pos 765 (T765) in OGT [4, 6]. Kinase-substrate specificity models predict gain of recognition at T765 by BMP/TGFβ-family receptor kinases ACVR1B, ACVR2A, ACVR2B, BMPR1A, and BMPR1B (gain events) [8]. This predicted kinase-rewiring could enable context-dependent phosphorylation at OGT T765 (to be tested by site-specific phospho readouts), thereby modulating OGT catalytic output and/or substrate selection, which is central to O-GlcNAc cycling in neurodevelopment [1, 2]. Because OGT-dependent O-GlcNAcylation can antagonize BMP signaling output in vivo (via OGT-mediated O-glycosylation of BMP pathway components), an acquired BMP/TGFβ-kinase input into OGT at T765 could plausibly create a maladaptive feedback between BMP/TGFβ signaling and O-GlcNAc cycling that contributes to neurodevelopmental phenotypes seen in OGT-CDG/X-linked ID [2, 3, 9].

Predicted observations

  • A765T-expressing cells show detectable phosphorylation at OGT T765 by targeted phosphoproteomics (PRM/SRM) and/or a custom pT765-OGT antibody, with signal increasing upon BMP/TGFβ pathway stimulation (e.g., BMP4 or activin A) and decreasing with ACVR/BMPR kinase inhibition (directionality to be empirically established) [8, 9].
  • Compared with WT OGT, A765T cells exhibit altered global O-GlcNAcylation (e.g., RL2/CTD110.6 immunoblot or O-GlcNAc proteomics), and the magnitude of this change is sensitive to ACVR/BMPR kinase perturbation, consistent with a newly introduced kinase-regulated control point [1, 2].
  • Mechanism-discriminating: disrupting the new phosphosite (A765T plus T765A reversion) abolishes BMP/activin-dependent changes in OGT functional readouts (global O-GlcNAc, specific neuronal substrate O-GlcNAcylation) without correcting any mutation-independent OGT defects, indicating causality of the phosphorylation node rather than generic missense destabilization [1, 8].
Laboratory validation

Suggested models

  • Human iPSC-derived neurons engineered to carry OGT A765T and isogenic WT control (CRISPR knock-in) [2].
  • Neuroblastoma or HEK293T cells with inducible WT vs A765T OGT for rapid phosphosite and signaling tests [1].

Assays

  • Targeted phosphoproteomics (PRM/SRM) across OGT residues 758–772 to quantify pT765 in WT vs A765T, with and without BMP4/activin A, and with and without ACVR/BMPR inhibitors [8].
  • Generate OGT variants: WT, A765T, A765T+T765A (phospho-null), and A765T+T765E (phosphomimetic proxy) and measure (i) global O-GlcNAc levels and (ii) OGT activity in lysates or purified protein where feasible [1, 5].
  • Kinase dependency test: knockdown/CRISPRi or pharmacologic inhibition of ACVR2A/ACVR2B/BMPR1A/BMPR1B and assess effects on pT765 signal and OGT functional outputs; perform rescue by re-expressing kinase-resistant or WT kinase as appropriate [8].
  • Epistasis/rescue: if A765T phenotypes (neurite outgrowth, synaptic marker expression, neuronal firing) are observed, test whether they are rescued by T765A but not by T765E, and whether BMP/activin pathway inhibition phenocopies T765A specifically in the A765T background [2, 3].

Controls and confounders

  • Control for OGT protein abundance and stability (cycloheximide chase) to separate phosphorylation-node effects from generic destabilization [5].
  • Control for global changes in cell state due to BMP/TGFβ stimulation by monitoring canonical pathway markers (e.g., pSMAD1/5/9 for BMP; pSMAD2/3 for activin/TGFβ) while interpreting OGT-specific effects [9].
  • Include OGA inhibition/perturbation controls to confirm that phenotypes depend on O-GlcNAc cycling rather than unrelated BMP responses [1, 2].

Feasibility notes

Because T765 is newly created by the variant, a custom phospho-specific antibody may be required; targeted MS on the 758–772 peptide provides an orthogonal route to validate site occupancy and stimulus dependence [8].

Limitations
  • Kinase events reflect predicted specificity rewiring; actual phosphorylation of OGT T765 and its directionality under BMP/activin conditions must be experimentally established [8].
  • Human relevance of ACVR/BMPR kinases phosphorylating a cytosolic/nuclear enzyme like OGT is plausible but not guaranteed; subcellular co-localization and kinase accessibility need confirmation [1].
  • The disease link for this exact variant (A765T) is not established in the provided sources beyond registry presence; the hypothesis targets a plausible OGT-CDG mechanism rather than proven pathogenicity of A765T [2, 3, 4].

Mechanistic expectations

OGT p.A765T creates a novel T765 phosphosite recognized by TGFβ/BMP receptors (ACVR1B/ACVR2A/ACVR2B/BMPR1A/BMPR1B), leading to context-dependent phosphorylation of OGT and altered O-GlcNAc cycling; the hypothesis is PTM-centric and does not require mRNA up/down but co-upregulation of receptor kinases in neurons could weakly support increased phosphorylation potential

Observed results

  • ACVR2A up (logFC 0.20, d 0.36, q 0.032)
  • BMPR1B borderline up (logFC 0.03, d 0.28, q 0.051)
  • ACVR1B down (logFC -0.18, d -0.41, q 0.031)
  • ACVR2B down (logFC -0.09, d -0.28, q 0.031)

Alignment assessment

Mixed receptor patterns (BMPR1A/ACVR2A up; ACVR1B/ACVR2B down) provide no coherent mRNA-level signature of uniformly increased TGFβ/BMP receptor capacity; OGT is modestly down in epilepsy, which neither confirms nor contradicts a PTM-based gain-of-phosphorylation mechanism; importantly, the disease context (epilepsy) is not OGT-CDG/neurodevelopmental tissue from affected individuals
