Overview

What the variant is and why it's being analyzed

FieldValue
GeneOGT
Mutation (CDS)
Mutation (AA)p.N648Y

OGT p.N648Y (ASN648TYR) has been reported as a de novo missense variant in OGT-CDG/XLID and associated with reduced OGT catalytic activity and decreased global O-GlcNAcylation [1, 2]. In our PTM analysis, N648Y creates a new central tyrosine phospho-acceptor at residue 648 that passes solvent-accessibility checks and shows a strong predicted gain in TNK1 tyrosine kinase specificity [4].

Hypotheses

Mechanistic explanations generated from the overview and literature.

OGT p.N648Y creates a de novo TNK1-targetable phosphotyrosine (Y648) that inhibits OGT function and contributes to OGT-CDG/XLID via reduced O-GlcNAcylation in neurons

Disease context

  • OGT-CDG / X-linked intellectual disability (XLID) [1, 2, 3]
